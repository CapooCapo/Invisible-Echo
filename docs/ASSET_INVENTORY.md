## Not integrated into production in this batch

- No third-party asset is used by the new night sky. Qwantani Night Pure Sky was downloaded as a 2K CC0 QA candidate after provenance/hash verification, then rejected because its cyan horizon remained too bright in the production view; it is documented as unused.
- Three dedicated project-authored restroom-fixture GLBs are present and used: toilet/cistern, basin/faucet, and urinal. Their Blender sources and reproducible generator are included. They are low-poly original models, not a finished architecture module or proof of visual acceptance.
- Existing licensed PSX School Kit files are not used by this WC pass. When used elsewhere, the attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms are recorded in `docs/ASSET_LICENSES.md`.

## Evidence limits

- Current GUI captures are staged production-scene views; they do not prove normal input-driven traversal, hide interaction, live mirror synchronization, continuous camera clearance, or collision-free fixture use.
- `builds/visual-qa/environment-recovery-20261016/wc_prompt_hud_after_bench.png` is a staged-position X11 capture of the actual main scene; it verifies the HUD/frame composition and wall-side bench placement only. Navigation is verified separately by `production_route_after_bench_reposition.log`.
- The mirror copy follows Hoàng's source model yaw and AnimationPlayer state, but its position and camera remain fixed in a separate staged SubViewport. Treat it as a stylized story apparition, not a physical, spatially synchronized reflection. The production first-person camera now aims at the physical mirror when An appears; keep overall visual acceptance `VISUAL_UNVERIFIED`.
