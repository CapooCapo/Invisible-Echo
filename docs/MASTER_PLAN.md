# Master Plan

## Definition of Done

Chapter 01's 15 supplied scenes are playable from menu to ending. All six keepsakes, six puzzle sequences, stealth/chase, legal chess, both endings, save/checkpoints, and the Night 2 unlock work; assets have verified licenses; desktop export and test evidence are documented. Night 2 story content is out of scope.

## Milestones

1. **Environment + vertical slice (in progress):** Godot project, controls and scenes 01–04. Acceptance: project imports and launches; letter interaction works; survival event enforces 3 seconds and retry; mirror objective appears.
2. **Shared systems:** player/camera, interaction, dialogue, inventory, objectives, save/checkpoints, menu/pause/settings.
3. **School mystery:** scenes 05–08; board clue, six-piece diary, 4×4 circuit, stealth patrol and escape; memories 1–3.
4. **Digital mystery:** scenes 09–11; 1804 access, camera ordering, evidence-based forum choice, deduction board; memories 4–6.
5. **Resolution:** scenes 12–15; return keepsakes, legal chess modes/retries/resign, three memories, branch-specific morning, Night 2 unlock.
6. **Art/audio:** consistent original or license-cleared school/character/prop/UI assets, Vietnamese typography, lighting and sound.
7. **QA/polish:** puzzle solvability, branch/save regression, performance and accessibility checks; playtest reports.
8. **Release:** Linux/Windows export verification, controls/README, asset credits and release checklist.

## Working rules

- Keep each milestone runnable and scoped; never mark it DONE on plan/code alone.
- Preserve original plot, names, six-item order, 1804 clue, choice consequences and endings.
- Prefer original code-drawn assets until suitable per-file license is verified.
- Keep temporary visuals explicitly labeled; no missing critical placeholder in release.
- Record engine output, test counts, visual-check status and blockers in `PROGRESS.md`.
